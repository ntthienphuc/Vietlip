## Accuracy and timing limits

The new real four-sentence phone session has WER 66.67% and CER 50.00%. The same
saved MP4 replay at frozen window times has WER 61.11%, both with and without the
experimental gate; these are different input paths and must not be subtracted
as a claimed improvement. Speech accuracy remains the principal limitation.

The new session's final caption render ACKs are 1.558–1.778 seconds after the
padded visual window endpoint. Acoustic speech end and MP4/camera clock offset
are not directly measured together. Earlier mapped prerecorded phone replay
estimated 1.79–2.04 seconds for two sentences; PC replay also contains a 3.50
second case. Therefore 2–3 seconds is a target, not a guaranteed response time.

The gate removes seven false inserted units in a controlled silence replay and
preserves all evaluated speech windows. The original silence failure does not
reproduce on the newer paced motion schedule; broad live improvement is not yet
established. The stable release keeps the baseline motion gate. See
`VISUAL_CONFIRMATION_AND_SCENE04_2026-11-10.md` for the full comparison and limits.
